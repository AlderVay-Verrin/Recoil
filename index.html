ORIGINAL ZERO-G ARENA SHOOTER

Recoil

Fight in open space with no up or down. Drift, dash, grab drifting cover, and use weapon recoil to push yourself through the arena.

WASD Thrust forward / back / sides
Mouse Look around
Space / C Thrust up / down
Q / E Roll left / right
Click Fire
RMB Aim and stabilize
Shift Directional dash
F Magnetic grab nearby cover
V Push nearby shots and enemies away
R Reload
1–6 Switch weapons
Tab / Esc Pause or release mouse
